## 📐 Email Writing Guidelines

### **Subject Line Best Practices:**

```
✅ Clear & specific
✅ Include emoji (1-2 max)
✅ Personalization (nama, kode donasi)
✅ Urgency (jika perlu, tapi jangan clickbait)
✅ Length: 40-60 karakter (optimal untuk mobile)

Examples:
✅ "✅ Konfirmasi Donasi - DON-20260617-001"
✅ "📊 Laporan Penyaluran Donasi - Kode [XXX]"
✅ "🌟 Update dari Yayasan ASIB - Donasi Sudah Tersalurkan!"
❌ "Penting!" (vague)
❌ "Buka sekarang juga!!!" (clickbait)
```

### **Email Structure:**

```
1. Greeting (Islamic + personal)
   "Assalamu'alaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh,
    Kak [Nama] yang dirahmati Allah,"

2. Opening (thank you/appreciation)
   "Terima kasih telah..."

3. Main Content (clear, structured)
   - Use bullet points
   - Short paragraphs
   - Bold important info

4. CTA (clear & specific)
   "Untuk pertanyaan, reply email ini atau..."

5. Closing (Islamic + warm)
   "Wassalamu'alaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh,
    Hormat kami,"

6. Signature (complete contact info)
```

### **Tone Guidelines:**

**DO:**
✅ Warm & friendly (seperti teman)  
✅ Professional tapi tidak kaku  
✅ Islamic values (Assalamu'alaikum, Aamiin, Jazakallah)  
✅ Empathetic & understanding  
✅ Clear & concise  
✅ Personal (gunakan nama)  

**DON'T:**
❌ Too formal/corporate  
❌ Too casual (no slang)  
❌ Preachy/condescending  
❌ Generic (tanpa personalization)  
❌ Too long (wall of text)  
❌ All caps (terkesan marah)
